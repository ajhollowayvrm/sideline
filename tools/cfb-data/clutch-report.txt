1. IS "CLUTCH" A REPEATABLE TEAM TRAIT?
   38.4% of all games are decided by 8 or fewer.

   year-over-year repeatability (season N → N+1):
     overall win%           r = 0.458   (n=528)
     blowout win%  (>8)     r = 0.482   (n=513)
     ONE-SCORE win% (≤8)    r = 0.078   (n=291)

   within one season, blowout win% vs one-score win%:  r = 0.193  (n=471)
   → being good repeats. Winning close games does not. There is no "clutch team" in the data;
     a model that lets a rating decide one-score games is modelling something that is not there.
